**“What if students use it only during festivals?”**

> That's why we are building daily utility—discussions, communities, Lost & Found, local discovery and campus conversations—not just event tooling.

**“How do you avoid becoming another dead campus app?”**

**“What if you have 1,000 colleges but only 20 users each?”**
